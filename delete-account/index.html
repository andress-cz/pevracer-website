Delete your PEV Racer account

To request deletion without the app, email support@pevracer.com from the address you used to register. Use the subject “Delete my PEV Racer account”. Support will verify account ownership before completing the request. Keep passwords and sign-in codes private.

In the app, open Social → Account → Delete account and confirm. This removes your sign-in, profile, cloud recordings, tracks and sharing relationships. Local rides, exported files and copies downloaded by other riders may remain. To erase the library on your phone, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings.

The privacy policy explains support and moderation records that may be retained.
