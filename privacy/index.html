PEV Racer privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Contact the PEV Racer operator at support@pevracer.com with questions or requests about your data.

Recording on your phone

PEV Racer uses Bluetooth to discover and connect to nearby GPS devices and vehicles. It stores selected device identifiers, ride timestamps, precise routes, speed, vehicle telemetry, tracks, laps and settings on your phone. Phone GPS can provide a live preview on the Devices screen or record your position during a ride you start. Recording can continue with the screen locked until you stop it. Android shows a recording notification when notifications are enabled.

You can record without an account. Videos you select for overlays are processed on your device. Android may back up or transfer the ride database and its recording files according to your system backup settings and size limits. These files include precise routes, tracks and vehicle telemetry. Authentication preferences, sign-in tokens and device settings are excluded from the app’s Android backup rules.

Maps and accounts

To display maps, the app requests map data from OpenFreeMap. The app and the website request satellite and aerial imagery from NASA GIBS, Terrascope (ESA WorldCover) and, for detailed views of Czechia, ČÚZK. These providers receive your IP address and the map tiles needed for the area you view. Phone GPS uses Android location services. If you register, the Supabase authentication service processes your email and password, issues sign-in tokens and sends account emails through the configured mail provider. The app keeps sign-in tokens in private app storage. Other signed-in riders can find your profile by login name or exact email. Your initial login name is based on the part of your email before the @ sign. You can change it in Social → Account.

Rider website

The website uses the same Supabase account and cloud library as Android. Your browser stores sign-in tokens so you can return to your dashboard. Sign out when using a shared computer. Opening a cloud session downloads the recording to your browser. Files you open for analysis and videos you select for overlays are processed locally. Choosing to save a recording or restore an Android backup uploads the selected ride data to your account. Changes you make on the website, such as titles, notes, laps, tracks and deletions, sync to your phone. A backup ZIP you download can contain precise routes and private notes.

Cloud sync and sharing

When you are signed in, the app syncs your ride recordings, routes, telemetry, lap results and tracks with your account in both directions. It syncs automatically on Wi-Fi, and on mobile data only if you allow it. When an item changed in both places, the later edit wins, and deleting a synced item on the phone or the website deletes it in both. Videos stay on your phone. Items begin as private. Sharing can give access to friends, selected riders, link recipients or signed-in riders more broadly. Entering rides in an event makes the recording available to event members. Joining a live event uploads matching laps and recordings while you ride. People who import or export shared data may retain their copies after you revoke access.

Exports, support and reports

With the Android file and share pickers, you choose where to send backups, videos and exported files. A device support report can include your phone model, Bluetooth name and address, raw device messages and your notes. You choose whether to send it. If you report community content, the service stores the report, your account identifier, the reported item and your explanation for moderation. Blocking a rider records the block on the service.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or clear app storage. Cloud copies remain until you delete the item or your account. Account deletion removes the account, its recordings, profile and associated cloud library. A support conversation or a de-identified moderation record may be retained to resolve abuse, handle disputes, or meet legal obligations. Copies retained by recipients, Android backups and files you export are managed separately. Contact support about these records or to request deletion.
